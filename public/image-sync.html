1 Dry run (read-only)

Nothing in the store changes here. Paste product handles, CFS product IDs (e.g. 144250) or SKUs (UD-144250-16985745), one per line — or leave empty to take the next batch of Urban Deco products.

Download preview CSV
2 Backup zip

Downloads a zip of the selected products' current photos plus a list of their order, alt text, featured photo and variant photos. Apply stays locked until the zip has downloaded and its checksum matches. Keep the zip for at least 30 days.

3 Apply

Per product: upload new photos → wait until Shopify has processed them → attach → fix alt text → reorder → set variant photos → detach removed photos (kept in Shopify Files for 7 days) → check the result. Products whose photos changed since the backup are skipped.

Final review — exactly what will be applied

Run the dry run and select products first.

Download what will be applied (CSV)
Download results (CSV)
4 Restore from backup

Upload a backup zip and pick products. Photos still in Shopify Files are re-attached; anything older is re-uploaded from the zip. Photos added after the backup are detached, never deleted. Image sync is blocked while a restore runs.

5 Clean-up (after results are accepted)

Lists photos the app detached. Only photos detached 7+ days ago, still in Shopify Files and not used by any product can be permanently deleted.
