1 Find

Lists every product with an option value "Default" or "Default Title" (Shopify's normal single products are left out) and suggests the real name from: the same SKU on another product, the CFS product name, or the product title (when it's the product's own CFS product). Nothing changes here.

2 Review & rename

A = "Default" next to real colours (customers see it — fix first) · B = only colour, product has sizes · C = one-variant product (usually hidden). Ticked by default: A and B with a high or medium suggestion, and merge rows. Edit any name before renaming.
Merge = the "Default" variants are the same CFS product as a real colour on the page (e.g. Bisque Oasis 2 Chairs + Default Title 4–12 Chairs) → they move into that colour and "Default" is removed. SKUs, prices, stock and photos stay as they are.
