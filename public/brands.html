1 Pick a brand

Each brand shows its Shopify products and variants (by vendor name) and the CFS feed's products and SKUs. 0 Shopify products means the vendor name on Shopify is different.

2 Stock update

For every Active Shopify product of this brand, copies from CFS: delivery time, stock status, on hand (→ quantity) and due date. SKUs not found in the brand's CFS feed are skipped and listed — they're for the clean-up step later.

Pick a brand first.
3 Product codes

Copies CFS codes onto this brand's Shopify products (all statuses), same as Urban Deco's Step 4: product_code on the product and variant_code on each variant. Only missing or different codes are written.

Pick a brand first.
4 Clean-up

Same as Urban Deco's Step 3, for this brand only: delete or draft variants that aren't in CFS, promote CFS product matches, remove the photos of deleted variants (backup zip first) and Undo. It opens in the main tool's Step 3 screen. Extra safety: anything not in this brand's feed is also checked against every other brand's feed — if it's found there, it's flagged, never deleted.

5 Order-ahead (pre-Christmas)

Lets customers order products that CFS has OUT OF STOCK but with stock due from today up to the cut-off date (a due date that has already passed doesn't count): sets stock_status_override = IN STOCK and quantity 5. The real stock status, due date and delivery time stay as CFS has them, so the store can say "order now — expected 17 Nov". When stock arrives, or the due date moves past the cut-off, it's switched off again — by this step or by the normal stock update. Active products only.
